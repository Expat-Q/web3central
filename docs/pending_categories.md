# Pending Categories for Web3Central

These categories were identified during the Arbitrum ecosystem onboarding and are not currently part of the core taxonomy.

## New High-Priority Categories
- **AI**: Artificial Intelligence agents, frameworks, and DeFAI protocols.
- **DePIN**: Decentralized Physical Infrastructure (Storage, Compute, Bandwidth).
- **Liquid Staking (LSTs)**: Protocols for liquid staking and restaking.
- **Payments**: Crypto cards, payroll, and invoicing solutions.

## Utility & Support Categories
- **Fiat On-Ramp**: Services bridging fiat to crypto.
- **Oracle**: Data feed providers and infrastructure.
- **Developer Tools**: SDKs, APIs, and dev-focused resources.
- **DAO Tools**: Governance and treasury management.
- **Infrastructure**: Node providers, RAAS, and core networking layers.

## Gaming Sub-categories
- **Action**
- **RPG** (Role Playing Games)
- **Strategy**
- **Casual**
- **Adventure**
- **GameFi**

---
*Note: During the initial onboarding, these dapps are being mapped to existing categories (e.g., AI -> DeFi/Trading, Payments -> DeFi) until these new categories are officially added to the UI.*
