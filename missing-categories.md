# Missing Categories for Web3Central

Based on an analysis of the current platform categories and the broader Web3 ecosystem (including Base and Arbitrum landscapes), the following categories are currently missing or could be expanded on Web3Central.

## 1. Infrastructure (Infra) & Dev Tools
Essential for the "Builder" side of Web3Central.
- **Sub-categories**: RPC Providers, Node Providers, SDKs, Indexers.
- **Example Protocols**: Alchemy, Infura, The Graph, Ankr, QuickNode.

## 2. Artificial Intelligence (AI)
The fastest-growing sector in Web3, focusing on agentic protocols and decentralized compute.
- **Sub-categories**: AI Agents, Decentralized LLMs, AI Compute, Prompt-to-Earn.
- **Example Protocols**: Bittensor, Morpheus, Fetch.ai, BrianKnows, Bitte Protocol.

## 3. Social & DeSoc (Social Finance)
Dedicated space for social graphs and censorship-resistant communication.
- **Sub-categories**: Social Media, Messaging, Content Platforms.
- **Example Protocols**: Farcaster, Lens Protocol, Friend.tech, AlfaFrens.

## 4. DAOs & Governance
Tools specifically for decentralized decision-making and treasury management.
- **Sub-categories**: Voting Platforms, Governance Aggregators, DAO Frameworks.
- **Example Protocols**: Snapshot, Tally, Aragon, Boardroom.

## 5. Identity & Names (DID)
Protocols that manage decentralized identity and human-readable addresses.
- **Sub-categories**: Domain Names, Soulbound Tokens, Proof of Personhood.
- **Example Protocols**: ENS, Worldcoin, Space ID, Unstoppable Domains.

## 6. DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure)
Real-world infrastructure powered by token incentives.
- **Sub-categories**: Decentralized Storage, Compute, Wireless Networks.
- **Example Protocols**: Filecoin, Arweave, Render Network, Helium.

## 7. Payments & Fiat On-ramps
Bridge between traditional finance and crypto for daily transactions.
- **Sub-categories**: Remittance, Invoicing, On-ramps/Off-ramps.
- **Example Protocols**: MoonPay, Transak, Bulla Network, MoonPay.

## 8. Staking & Liquid Staking (LST)
A major part of the DeFi ecosystem that deserves its own focus beyond general "DeFi".
- **Sub-categories**: Liquid Staking, Restaking, Yield Aggregators.
- **Example Protocols**: Lido, Rocket Pool, EigenLayer, Jito.

## 9. Data & Oracles
The "middleware" of Web3 that feeds real-world data to smart contracts.
- **Sub-categories**: Price Feeds, Verifiable Randomness (VRF), Data Analytics.
- **Example Protocols**: Chainlink, Pyth Network, API3, Redstone.

## 10. Education & Learning (Learn-to-Earn)
Platforms that reward users for learning about the ecosystem.
- **Sub-categories**: Educational dApps, Certification Protocols.
- **Example Protocols**: Bankless Academy, AnyCampus, RabbitHole.

## 11. Launchpads & Fundraising
Platforms for initial token offerings and community bootstrapping.
- **Sub-categories**: IDO Platforms, Crowdfunding.
- **Example Protocols**: PinkSale, DAO Maker, Polkastarter.

## 12. Explorers & Utilities
Core tools for interacting with and verifying blockchain activity.
- **Sub-categories**: Block Explorers, Gas Trackers, MEV Tools.
- **Example Protocols**: Etherscan, Blockscout, Gas.zip.
